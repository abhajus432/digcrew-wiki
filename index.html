How these numbers work
  • The chain. Iron and copper: raw ore → crusher (about ×2.3) → smelter (×1.6) → paid by the train (+10%). Gold and platinum: wash plant → Gold Room (+20%) → train (+10%).
  • Per scoop or per truck, not per hour. A total like 100k is one full truck.
  • Upgrades. Each Capacity level adds +15% to the bucket or the truck body, 3 levels at most (+45%).
  • Crushing line. Jaw ≈ ×2.3, with a cone crusher ≈ ×2.45, with a screen ≈ ×2.6.
  • Market. Everything follows today's IRON / COPPER / GOLD market %.

How deep should I dig?

Your HUD shows a line like “Iron ore 0.2 m below · 2.5 m thick”. It means: the ore starts 0.2 m under your bucket and is 2.5 m thick. Leave the boxes empty for a plan from untouched ground (the game's own layer depths for this lot), or type your HUD's two numbers for the spot you are on.

When the rig strikes, the game tells you what the well is worth — e.g. “STRUCK OIL at 918 m - a good well, about 0.43 m³ a minute” — and the wellhead shows the same: “Wellhead · good well · 0.43 m³/min”.

Where to drill
Green flags only. The grey-ground well measured 0.11 m³ a minute — about a fifth of a good one, roughly $18,000 an hour new — and amber (orange) ground is not worth digging either: you pay the same diesel, 10 minutes and $350,000 jack for a smaller well. Expect to take whatever the green spot gives, between 0.43 and 0.53.
The Reed Swamp and Black Swamp are not measured yet. Their crude pays more ($4,500 and $7,500 a m³) and the hope is that their wells turn out steadier than the Dry Flats, but that is a hope, not data.

3 · What a jack earns

New well vs settled well
New (first rate): right after you put a jack on a well it pumps its full rate — the number in the install message, e.g. “pumping about 0.48 m³ a minute”.
Settled: over time the well eases down to 60% of that first rate and then stays there for good — it never runs dry (game text). A well that starts at 0.48 m³ a minute ends up at about 0.29 (one jack we watched went from 0.48 to about 0.39 after it had been running a while).
So the new figure is what you make in the first stretch, the settled figure is what a well makes for the rest of its life — use settled to plan.
How long the easing takes is not in the game’s files (it runs on the server). To see it on your wells: note the jack’s m³/min (its prompt, or the tank read-out) now and again a few hours later.
Every well pumps a different amount — the wellhead shows its grade and rate as soon as the rig strikes, before you buy the jack. The survey flag under the well matters (green good, amber fair, grey poor), but the game's exact rule is not public, so the money figures here use the measured Dry Flats range of 0.43–0.53 m³ a minute, not a promise. Wells ease down to 60% of their first rate and never run dry (game text). Quick calculator: Lot guides › #13.

5 · Working in the mud

  • Road tyres sink. Truck & dogs, low-loaders, road tippers and haul trucks bog down when more than half loaded. Utes, loaders and the articulated dump truck get through under half a load; tracked machines cope, slowly.
  • Stuck? Tip some of the load, tow it out (HOOK TOW CHAIN on foot), or lay a gravel road across with the paver.
  • Swamp Long Reach 200T ($2.6M): wide pads float on the mud — no bogging, no slowing. Its long boom digs the swamp out from the bank. Moves only on the Mega Haul Rig.
  • Pits flood. Dig below the swamp's water and the pit fills about 0.6 m a minute until it is level with the swamp. Bank the pit in first (a ring of ground higher than the water all round), then pump it with the Dewatering Truck — a pit still open to the swamp keeps filling and won't pump down.
  • Roads: the paver lays a gravel road from washed gravel (wash plant by-product); the roller seals it at $10 a m².

Getting started — first loads

  • Your yard starts with a bulldozer, excavator and dump truck. The tutorial walks your first loads with a marker to follow.
  • Push the topsoil off Lot 1 with the bulldozer until the orange iron ore shows, then dig it with the excavator into the truck. Plain dirt doesn't sell.
  • The Quick Sale Bin is fast cash but only a quarter of the Railyard price — the Railyard ore buyer pays 4× the bin, so haul there once you can.
  • Buy lots in COMPANY > PERMITS, or at the LEASE AVAILABLE gate on a lot's fence.
  • Build plants in PLANTS & MARKET: the Crusher → concentrate (~2.4×), the Wash Plant washes pay-dirt for gold (and platinum from the swamps), the Smelter → pig metal (×1.6 +10%).
  • Machines burn diesel — keep a fuel trailer handy.
  • Hired drivers run your trucks (FLEET > SILO RUNS trucks a plant's silo to the Railyard; CUSTOM ROUTE drives a route you set) — the biggest $/hour jump.
  • In-game, press ? next to any plant or machine to open its own help page.

Legacy — selling your company (rebirth)

New in the latest update (patch notes 31, Sell the Company): once your company is worth $25,000,000 you can sell it (COMPANY > ★ LEGACY) and start a new firm with a Legacy ★ — +10% on everything you sell, a perk point to spend and a gold star over your head. Company value is cash + fleet + plants (checked against the game's own figure). The LEGACY board at the Railyard, beside Top Earners, ranks them.
